# Playground

Every `<klappay-button>` combination on the page backgrounds a merchant's
checkout is likely to have — each one is the real element, rendered by the
package, not a screenshot. Use it to pick the variant that contrasts with
your page: `black` on a light page, `white` on a dark one.

The last button in each row has no `charge-id`, so it renders in its
disabled state. `origin` points at a placeholder domain, so clicking
opens nothing real.
